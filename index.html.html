About me

Crafting short-form content that people actually watch

I'm Mahmoud El-Dawy, a senior video editor and content creator with 5+ years of experience, specializing in short-form content. Every edit starts with one question: why would someone keep watching? From the first second's hook to the final frame, I build videos around pacing, story and retention.
